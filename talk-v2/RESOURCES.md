# Math 2.0: resources

Links from the talk *Math 2.0: From Society to the Lab* (Kyle Poe, Vidal Lab, University of Pennsylvania, November 2026). Every link was checked on 11 November 2026.

## Learn to read and write Lean

- [Mathematics in Lean](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathematics_in_lean/): the standard introduction for mathematicians.
- [Theorem Proving in Lean 4](https://lean-lang.org/theorem_proving_in_lean4/): the language itself.
- [Natural Number Game](https://adam.math.hhu.de/#/g/leanprover-community/nng4): a first hour of Lean, in the browser.
- [Mathlib documentation](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/): search for what is already formalized.
- [Lean Zulip](https://leanprover.zulipchat.com/): where the community answers questions.

## Blueprints: statements and proofs as a graph

- [leanblueprint](https://github.com/PatrickMassot/leanblueprint) (Massot): write the informal proof in LaTeX, linked to Lean.
- [LeanArchitect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22554) (Zhu, Monticone, Avigad, Welleck): generate the blueprint from Lean code.
- [Verso Blueprint](https://github.com/leanprover/verso-blueprint) (Lean FRO): blueprints native to Lean.
- [Lean Atlas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16347) (Yanahama & Sannai): find the nodes a person has to check.
- [Formalizing the proof of PFR in Lean4 using Blueprint: a short tour](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2023/11/18/formalizing-the-proof-of-pfr-in-lean4-using-blueprint-a-short-tour/) (Tao, 2023).

## Proving and checking

- [Comparator](https://github.com/leanprover/comparator) (Lean FRO): check that a proof proves exactly the stated theorem.
- [lean-lsp-mcp](https://github.com/oOo0oOo/lean-lsp-mcp): connect an AI agent to Lean.
- [Prove2Me](https://prove2.me/about): human-written statements, agent-written proofs, organized as a graph.
- [Palomar](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/08/18/palomar-a-registry-of-lean-verified-mathematics/): a registry of Lean-verified mathematics (Tao's announcement).

## Learning theory in Lean

- [SLT: lean-stat-learning-theory](https://github.com/YuanheZ/lean-stat-learning-theory) (Zhang, Lee & Liu, ICML 2026).
- [StatsMLlib](https://statsmllib.github.io/).
- [CvxLean](https://github.com/verified-optimization/CvxLean) (Bentkamp, Fernández Mir & Avigad, TACAS 2023).

## Reading

- W. P. Thurston, [On proof and progress in mathematics](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9404236), Bull. AMS 30(2), 1994.
- I. Lakatos, *Proofs and Refutations*, Cambridge University Press, 1976.
- Reisinger et al., [The Mathematical Sciences in an AI-enabled World](https://www.acadmathsci.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Mathematical_Sciences_AI_AcadMathsSci_Oct2026_preprint_final.docx.pdf), Academy for the Mathematical Sciences, October 2026 (pre-publication draft).
- [Proofs and Prompts](https://proofsandprompts.com/): essays by mathematicians on how AI is changing their practice.
